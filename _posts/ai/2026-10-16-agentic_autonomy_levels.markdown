AI agents can work at different levels of autonomy. Here are six levels, from manual work to fully autonomous systems. Each level has its place in a workflow.

![Agentic Autonomy Levels](/assets/img/ai/agentic_autonomy_levels.jpg)

- **Level 0**: The agent is a research tool. You ask, it answers, you decide. No automation.
- **Level 1**: The agent generates drafts and suggestions. You review and edit everything before it takes effect.
- **Level 2**: The agent acts on well-scoped tasks with your approval on each action.
- **Level 3**: The agent acts on defined tasks without approval, but reports what it did afterwards.
- **Level 4**: The agent acts, reports what it did, and you can override only in rare cases.
- **Level 5**: The agent is fully autonomous. It makes all decisions and acts without oversight.

Each level trades autonomy for control. **Level 1 and 2 are safest for high-stakes work**. **Level 3 and 4 work for routine tasks**. **Level 5 is rare and should only run unattended on isolated systems**.

The highest autonomy is not always the best. Choose the level that fits your risk tolerance and the task.
